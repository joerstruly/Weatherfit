## Highest-value systematic enumeration levers found

1. **`leegov.com/procurement/Project%20Documents/&lt;BID FOLDER&gt;/`** — Lee County FL publishes raw vendor catalogs with literal LIST/DISCOUNT/NET columns across water/sewer, electrical (Graybar), paint (Sherwin-Williams), and manufacturer list books (T-Christy). Best single corpus found.

6. **NYC DCAS year-indexed bid-tab pages → `nyc.gov/assets/dcas/downloads/pdf/business/bidtabs/&lt;EPIN&gt;.pdf`**.
