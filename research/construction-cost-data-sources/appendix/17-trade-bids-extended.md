# Trade-level bid results — extended pass (Opus agent; budget exhausted mid-Task D)

## TWO SYSTEMIC UNLOCKS
1. **HUD Handbook 7460.8 Ch. 6** (https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/74608c06pihh.pdf): as bids are publicly opened and read aloud, an **abstract (tabulation) of all bids must be prepared showing each bidder's name and prices including alternates**. Every HUD-funded public housing authority therefore *has* an all-bidder abstract with alternates whether or not it posts one. Records requests are a reliable fallback across all PHAs nationally.
2. **Illinois 30 ILCS 500 Art. 30** (https://law.justia.com/codes/illinois/chapter-30/act-30-ilcs-500/article-30/): on **single-prime** projects where a public higher-ed institution is the construction agency and the contract exceeds **$250,000**, the low bidder's bid **must identify the subcontractor name AND the bid proposal cost for each of the five subdivisions**. Trade prices are disclosed even without multi-prime award. Time-limited through 12/31/2026.
