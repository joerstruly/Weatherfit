# Commercial & multifamily material buying channels — price signal map (Opus agent; budget hit at 200)

Tags: VERIFIED-URL / VERIFIED-DETAIL / UNVERIFIED / LOW-CONF (SEO or AI cost-guide sites; do not benchmark against).

## Headline findings
1. The real contractor channel is **distributor net pricing off a manufacturer list price book**, and the list books are far more public than expected: Watts, Viega (XLSX), Charlotte Pipe, NIBCO, Uponor, Tyler Pipe, Southwire, Cerrowire, Eaton (PAD + B-Line XLSX), Schneider DigestPLUS, Allegion, ASSA ABLOY (via SECLOCK), Lawrence Hardware publish dated list-price files openly.
2. The best **public net-price** signal is public-agency procurement: county/city annual materials bids publish literal List / Discount / Net columns from Ferguson, Graybar etc.; state term contracts (NY OGS) publish discount-off-list schedules; GSA Advantage price lists are fully public.
3. **Distributor price-increase-letter archives** (ABC Supply low-slope, ABC Supply Interiors/L&W, SRS, FBM, Cameron Ashley, Negwer, Marjam, KAMCO, Mid-Atlantic Roofing, Carolina Atlantic) are a continuously updated public record of manufacturer price levels and effective dates for drywall, framing, insulation, roofing.
4. Licensable feeds are a duopoly per trade: electrical/mechanical = Trimble Trade Service (TRA-SER) and Vision InfoSoft EPIC; plumbing/mechanical = Harrison Publishing House (CINX). ~$450–$1,800/yr.
